# Sales Performance & Profitability Analytics

##  Power BI Portfolio Project

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance,
profitability, customer behavior, shipping efficiency, and discount scenarios.

![Dashboard Preview](screenshots/executive-overview.png)

##  Business Problem

Sales revenue alone does not provide a complete picture of business performance.
This project analyzes transactional sales data to identify revenue drivers,
profitability issues, customer value, operational delays, and discount impacts.

##  Business Questions

- Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which categories are most profitable?
- Where are the loss-making products?
- How does discounting affect profitability?
- Which customers generate the greatest value?
- Where are shipping delays concentrated?
- What happens to estimated profit when discounts change?

##  Key Insights

- Sales performance should be evaluated alongside profitability, as high sales do not always result in high profit.
- Higher discount levels can reduce profitability and should be monitored carefully.
- Loss-making products require further investigation to identify the causes of negative profit.
- A smaller group of high-value customers can contribute significantly to overall sales, highlighting the importance of customer retention.
- Repeat customer behavior provides an important indicator for evaluating customer retention.
- Shipping performance varies across regions and shipping modes, creating opportunities for operational improvement.
- Sales trends reveal periods of stronger and weaker demand that can support seasonal planning.
- The What-If simulator demonstrates the potential profitability impact of changing discount levels.

##  Business Recommendations

1. Review products with high discounts and low or negative profit.
2. Investigate and address consistently loss-making products.
3. Focus sales strategies on profitable growth rather than revenue alone.
4. Develop targeted retention strategies for high-value and repeat customers.
5. Investigate regions and shipping modes with higher delivery delays.
6. Use historical sales trends to improve inventory and promotional planning.
7. Use the What-If simulator to evaluate discount strategies before implementation.
